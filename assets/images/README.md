# Site images

One folder per page. Drop a page's final photos into its folder and they can be
wired into that page and published.

| Folder            | Page                                  |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|
| `home/`           | Home                                  |
| `about/`          | About Us                              |
| `services/`       | Services and the four service pages   |
| `careers/`        | Careers                               |
| `certifications/` | Certifications                        |
| `contact/`        | Contact Us                            |
| `privacy-policy/` | Privacy Policy                        |

Shared images stay where they are: `hero/` (the banner photo at the top of each
page, plus the home slideshow) and `logo/`.

Photos around 1600px wide, saved as JPG, keep pages fast to load.
